Her Story

Decades of Expertise.
Lifelong Commitment.

Sigal Fishman has been a trusted real estate professional since 1985, bringing decades of market expertise, strategic negotiation skills, and an unwavering commitment to her clients. With a direct, honest, and results-driven approach, Sigal has built her reputation on identifying value, negotiating the strongest terms, and guiding transactions smoothly from contract to closing.

Her extensive experience spans residential sales, luxury properties, investment opportunities, and helping buyers and sellers navigate changing market conditions with confidence.

"Aggressive when it counts, strategic by nature, and committed to excellence. There is no better real estate agent than Sigal." — Longtime Client

Specializing in Boca Raton, Highland Beach, Delray Beach, and luxury South Florida real estate. Sigal's current clients include relocating families from the Northeast, cash investors, and long-time South Florida residents.
