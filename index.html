Meet Sigal

Decades of Expertise.
Lifelong Commitment.

Sigal Fishman has been a trusted real estate professional since 1985, bringing decades of market expertise, strategic negotiation skills, and an unwavering commitment to her clients. With a direct, honest, and results-driven approach, she has built her reputation on identifying value, negotiating the strongest terms, and guiding transactions smoothly from contract to closing.

Her extensive experience spans residential sales, luxury properties, and investment opportunities — helping buyers and sellers navigate changing market conditions with confidence.

"Aggressive when it counts, strategic by nature, and committed to excellence — there is no better real estate professional than Sigal."

Sigal holds both a BA and an MBA from leading Boston universities and built a successful corporate career at Citizens Financial Group, Fidelity Investments, and Staples. This rare business background gives her clients a unique advantage in pricing, positioning, and negotiating.
