Market guide · Late 2026

Parkland is Sigal’s north Broward market: gated communities, golf, and large-lot family homes west of Boca Raton and Coral Springs. Buyers come here for schools, privacy, and square footage — not a walkable downtown.

Clients who start in Boca and want more land, a quieter street, or a golf-community lifestyle often end up touring Heron Bay, Parkland Golf & Country Club, MiraLago, and Pinehurst in the same weekend.

1. How the Parkland housing market has shifted in 2026

2026 in Parkland is a functioning, negotiated market. Inventory is healthier than the frenzy years. Buyers who understand HOA dues, golf memberships, and lot drainage win. Sellers who pretend it is still 2021 do not.

  • Family demand holds. Schools and gated living still pull relocating families from the Northeast and from Boca who want more lot. That is the point of the market.
  • Days on market. Around 65 days. Updated golf-course and lakefront homes beat tired interiors that skip inspection realities.
  • Buyer leverage. On houses that need roofs, impact glass, or membership transfers, 2026 buyers can negotiate. Turnkey still sells closer to ask.

2. Is it a buyer’s or seller’s market?

Parkland’s 2026 dynamic is less “luxury vs. condo” than “golf community vs. non-golf” and “updated vs. original.”

3. New construction & what’s being built

Parkland’s 2026 growth is infill and rebuilds inside existing gated communities — not West Boca–style brand-new master plans at this scale.

  • Parkland Golf & Country Club. Estates and golf-course product that still defines the top of the market.
  • Heron Bay & MiraLago. Established gated living with a mix of original and fully renovated homes — the typical 2026 “new” house is a rebuilt or heavily updated original.
  • Custom lots. Scattered new construction for buyers who want a specific floor plan rather than a builder tract.

4. What makes Parkland a magnet for buyers?

Schools & family living

A primary reason relocating families choose Parkland over a denser Boca pocket or a beach condo.

Gated golf communities

Parkland Golf & Country Club, Heron Bay, and MiraLago — privacy and amenities without East Boca waterfront pricing.

Lot size

Larger lots and custom homes are the product. This is not a downtown-loft market.

Location in the metro

West of Boca and Coral Springs, with a practical shot to Palm Beach County and Fort Lauderdale. Commutes and airport access are part of the pitch.

Frequently asked questions

What is the median home price in Parkland in 2026?

This site’s working median is about $1.1M, with a typical band from the high-$700s into several million for golf-course and custom estates.

Is Parkland a good alternative to Boca Raton?

If you want gated communities, larger lots, and Broward schools west of Boca, yes. It is not a beach town. It is a different market Sigal can compare side by side.

What should buyers watch for in 2026?

HOA and golf memberships, insurance, and lot drainage. The deals are in the details, not in a citywide median.

Are there good schools and amenities?

Schools are a primary reason families choose Parkland. Tour the specific community — Heron Bay, Parkland Golf & Country Club, MiraLago, and Pinehurst each live differently.
