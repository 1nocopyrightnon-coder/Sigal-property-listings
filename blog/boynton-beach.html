Market guide · Late 2026

Boynton Beach is Palm Beach County’s value coastal city: south of Lake Worth, north of Delray, with a working marina downtown and a deep bench of golf, 55+, and family neighborhoods inland.

In 2026 it often moves faster than Boca or Highland Beach. That is not hype — it is a lower median, more housing stock, and buyers who are done losing bids in Delray.

1. How the Boynton Beach housing market has shifted in 2026

Boynton entered late 2026 as one of the more liquid markets Sigal covers. Days on market here have run shorter than the luxury beach towns. Pricing still has to be local — ocean condos, marina, and west golf are not one chart.

  • Days on market. Around 54 days on this site’s snapshot — quicker than Boca’s 75–99 day band. Well-priced inland homes can be faster still.
  • Inventory. More houses to choose from than Highland Beach or East Boca. That helps buyers. It also punishes sellers who list at Delray-adjacent fantasy numbers.
  • Who is buying. Relocators stretching a Delray budget, 55+ buyers, and families who want Palm Beach County schools and amenities without Mizner Park pricing.

3. New construction & what’s being built

Boynton’s 2026 pipeline is a mix of established community resales and ongoing west / 55+ development — not Boca’s branded-tower story.

  • 55+ and amenitized west. Communities in the Valencia family and peers keep drawing relocators who want club living without a Boca West equity check.
  • Downtown & harbor. Incremental mixed-use and residential around Boynton Harbor Marina — lifestyle, not high-volume luxury towers.
  • Oceanfront condos. Mostly resale. Condition and building health beat “new construction” marketing that does not exist on every parcel.

4. What makes Boynton Beach a magnet for buyers?

Palm Beach County value

A lower median than Delray and Boca with the same county, beaches, and tax structure.

Marina & downtown

Boynton Harbor is a real waterfront downtown — boats, dining, and a local core.

Golf & 55+ depth

Quail Ridge, Hunters Run, Valencia Isles — options Boca buyers often tour when the budget says “not East Boca.”

Speed

Shorter days on market, in this snapshot, than the trophy beach towns. Liquidity is a feature.

Frequently asked questions

What is the median home price in Boynton Beach in 2026?

This site’s working median is about $399K, with ocean and golf communities running well above that and value condos below.

Why are homes selling faster here than in Boca?

More inventory at reachable prices, and a buyer pool that includes people priced out of Delray and East Boca. Micro-markets still vary.

Is Boynton Beach a good investment?

For the right product — yes. Underwrite HOA, insurance, and whether you are buying lifestyle (marina) or yield (inland). Sigal will not blur those.

Should I look at Boynton if I started in Delray?

Often. Many buyers do both. The downtowns are different; the budgets usually decide.
