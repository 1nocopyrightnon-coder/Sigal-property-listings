Market guide · Late 2026

Delray Beach sits just north of Boca Raton with a different personality: a downtown people actually use. Atlantic Avenue, Pineapple Grove, and the public beach set the tempo — then the market fans west into golf, 55+, and family neighborhoods.

If you are comparing Delray with Boca in 2026, the question is rarely “which is better.” It is which lifestyle you want to pay for — walkable coastal energy versus Boca’s broader luxury and club inventory.

1. How the Delray Beach housing market has shifted in 2026

Like the rest of coastal Palm Beach County, Delray cooled from the frenzy years into a more orderly 2026. Buyers can tour. Sellers still succeed when they respect downtown vs. west pricing — not a single citywide number.

  • Inventory. More listings than 2021–2022 means fewer panic offers. The best streets near the Avenue still move; dated product inland waits.
  • Days on market. Around 72 days on average. Walk-to-beach and renovated downtown product often beats that; over-improved west Delray can sit longer.
  • Negotiation. Condos and older townhomes leave more room. Turnkey single-family near the coast still sees multiple serious buyers when priced in the first week.

2. Is it a buyer’s or seller’s market?

Delray’s 2026 market splits cleanly by product and distance from the ocean.

3. New construction & what’s being built

Delray does not have Boca’s master-planned west boom. 2026 construction is infill, downtown, and selective west communities.

  • Downtown & beach. Low- and mid-rise infill, boutique condos, and renovated historic fabric around Atlantic Avenue and Pineapple Grove.
  • Intracoastal & Tropic Isle. Tear-downs and custom rebuilds still define the waterfront story — limited lots, high finishes, patient buyers.
  • West Delray. Amenitized and 55+ product continues to attract relocators who want Palm Beach County without East Boca pricing.

4. What makes Delray Beach a magnet for buyers?

Atlantic Avenue

Dining, nightlife, and a real main street — rare for South Florida, and a reason second-home and primary buyers pay a premium.

Arts & Pineapple Grove

Galleries, events, and a walkable arts district that keeps downtown busy beyond tourist season.

Beach town, not a high-rise strip

Public beach and a human-scale downtown — denser than Highland Beach, livelier than Boynton’s core.

Between Boca and the County line

Easy access to Boca employers, Brightline (via Boca), and the rest of Palm Beach County without giving up Delray’s character.

Frequently asked questions

What is the median home price in Delray Beach in 2026?

This site’s working median is about $520K, with a wide band from the mid-$300s to several million on the water. East of I-95 and walk-to-Avenue streets sit well above the citywide median.

Is Delray Beach a good rental or second-home market?

Yes, especially near downtown and the beach — with the usual caveat: confirm HOA and city short-term rental rules before you underwrite income.

How does Delray compare with Boca Raton?

Delray is more walkable and social; Boca is larger, more gated-club, and more luxury inventory. Many of Sigal’s clients tour both in the same week.

Is 2026 a good time to sell in Delray?

If your home shows well and is priced to the micro-market (Avenue vs. west), yes. Citywide averages hide how different those two Delrays are.
