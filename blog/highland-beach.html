Market guide · Late 2026

Highland Beach is only a few miles long, and that is the point. Tucked between Boca Raton and Delray Beach, it has no commercial strip, no Atlantic Avenue, and no Mizner Park. What it has is ocean, privacy, and a housing stock that is almost entirely condo towers and single-family estates.

In 2026, that scarcity still sets the rules. There is not much land left. Buyers who want this lifestyle rarely substitute a west Boca golf tract and call it the same thing.

1. How the Highland Beach housing market has shifted in 2026

Highland Beach followed the broader coastal slowdown — more days on market, more inspection time — without becoming a discount ocean town. The 2026 story is patience and condition, not fire sales.

  • Thin inventory. There are never “many” Highland Beach listings. A handful of well-located units can make the whole town feel busy — or quiet.
  • Days on market. Around 90 days on average. Direct-ocean, renovated residences still outperform dated interiors in the same building.
  • Pricing discipline. Ask prices that ignore insurance, assessments, and view corridors sit. Realistic oceanfront still finds a qualified buyer.

2. Is it a buyer’s or seller’s market?

Almost the entire town is luxury coastal product. The split is building vs. sand, and turnkey vs. original.

3. New construction & what’s being built

Highland Beach is largely built out. 2026 “new construction” here means renovations, rare teardowns, and the occasional condo redevelopment — not master-planned communities.

  • Condo renovations. Kitchens, impact glass, and lobby/amenity upgrades are how buildings compete. Buyers pay for turnkey, not potential.
  • Estate rebuilds. A small number of lots support custom ocean or near-ocean homes. Entitlements and neighbors matter; this is not West Boca lot inventory.
  • Why so little new supply. No commercial zoning and a built barrier-island footprint. Scarcity is the product.

4. What makes Highland Beach a magnet for buyers?

No commercial zoning

No strip malls, no nightlife corridor. The town stays residential — that is the amenity.

Direct Atlantic access

Beach living without sharing it with a downtown. Boca and Delray are minutes away when you want restaurants.

Privacy

A 2.5-mile enclave. You are not lost in a 90,000-person city. You are in a beach town with a Boca/Delray address-adjacent lifestyle.

Long-term scarcity

You cannot annex a new Highland Beach. That supports values for owners who buy the right building or lot.

Frequently asked questions

What is the median home price in Highland Beach in 2026?

This site’s working median is about $1.8 million, with condos and estates stretching from the high hundreds of thousands into the tens of millions for trophy oceanfront.

Are Highland Beach condos a buyer’s market in 2026?

Older towers with rising insurance and assessments, yes — relative to 2021. Direct-ocean, renovated residences are still selective. Underwrite the building, not just the unit.

Is Highland Beach good for a primary home or a seasonal home?

Both. Many owners are seasonal. Confirm rental rules, reserves, and whether the building fits a full-time or snowbird calendar.

How is it different from Boca Raton?

Boca has golf, downtown, and west master-plans. Highland Beach is almost purely ocean living. Sigal treats them as neighboring — not interchangeable — markets.
